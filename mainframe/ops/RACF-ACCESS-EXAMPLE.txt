Purpose: restrict access to expense datasets and load libraries to authorized users.

Ask the authorized mainframe administrator to create or approve profiles.

Illustrative command pattern (not ready-to-run; replace profile/group names):
  RDEFINE DATASET 'YOURHLQ.EXPENSE.**' UACC(NONE)

Operational guidance:
- Give write/update access only to the job owner or approved processing group.
- Restrict access to transaction, employee-master, rejection, and report datasets.
- Keep job logs and access decisions for audit review.
- Never put passwords or real personal/financial records in source files or screenshots.
- These commands require appropriate RACF authority and must be reviewed by the system administrator.
